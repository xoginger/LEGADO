# Code of Conduct (short)

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in LEGADO a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

**Examples of positive behavior**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for families using the project

**Unacceptable behavior**

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others’ private information without consent
- Sexualized or violent language/imagery in project spaces

## Enforcement

Report issues to the maintainers via the project’s issue tracker or the contact published in the repository. Maintainers may warn, remove contributions, or ban participants who violate this code.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
